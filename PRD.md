# OneMetric PRD

## Product Overview

OneMetric is an all-in-one analytics platform for indie hackers and SaaS founders.

The goal is to provide simple and affordable analytics.

---

# Version 1 (MVP)

Only implement Version 1.

## Website Analytics

Track:

- Visitors
- Sessions
- Page views
- Unique visitors
- Session duration
- Bounce rate

Display:

- Top pages
- Top referrers
- Countries
- Devices
- Browsers

---

## Event Tracking

Support custom events:

- Signup
- Login
- Purchase
- Button clicks
- Form submissions

---

## Funnels

Allow users to create funnels.

Example:

Landing Page
→ Pricing Page
→ Signup
→ Payment

Display:

- Conversion rates
- Drop-offs

---

## Revenue Attribution

Integrations:

- PayPal

Display:

- Revenue by source
- Revenue by campaign

---

## Weekly Reports

Generate weekly email summaries.

Use templates.

No AI.

---

## Installation

Single JavaScript snippet.

Installation should take less than one minute.

---

# Success Criteria

- First paying customers.
